A Short Introduction

About

I like learning across disciplines, and connecting ideas that normally live far apart. That's the honest throughline, more than any single job title — physics taught me to ask how something works underneath; machine learning gave me tools to go looking for the answer in data; and poetry is where I put whatever the first two couldn't quite hold.

I studied Engineering Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, which is a strange enough degree that it forces you to sit with both the precision of physics and the mess of everything it touches. From there I moved toward AI and data science — not by abandoning that curiosity, but by pointing it at new kinds of problems: how a model can be pushed to serve a population it was quietly underserving, how a paper's claims hold up once you actually try to reproduce them, how millions of scattered opinions on a pandemic add up to something you can study.

Writing came earlier than any of that, and it came from my father. Poetry has stayed with me the way a habit stays with you — not always visible, but never really gone — through slice-of-life narratives and the slower, quieter work of sitting with memory and longing. Ivied Walls, my debut collection, is the first time that habit became something someone else could hold in their hands.

I don't think of research and poetry as opposite ends of anything. Both are just close attention, pointed in different directions.
